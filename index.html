MY PROCESS

How I Solve & Design

01 - Discover

First, I dive deep to understand what's really happening. I explore user needs, business goals, and challenges to uncover core insights.

02 - Define

Next, I clarify objectives by defining key metrics, data needs, and success criteria. I map data flows and ensure alignment with business needs.

03 - Ideate

I brainstorm possible analytical approaches evaluating different techniques to address business questions effectively.

04 - Design

This is where insights start to take shape. I design and build ETL workflows, and create dashboards to visualize findings.

05 - Test & Iterate

I validate data accuracy, gather feedback from users. Iteration ensures usability, performance, and alignment with business needs.

06 - Build & Refine

I collaborate with cross-functional teams to deploy solutions at scale, ensuring performance and reliability in production.
